# Outreach Scripts

Replace `[brackets]`. Make every message personal, with one specific detail about *them*.

## 1. Warm DM / text (friends, ex-colleagues, LinkedIn connections)

> Hey [Name], quick one. I'm taking on 4 businesses this week to automate one painful admin task each (quote follow-ups, invoice chasing, lead intake, review requests). Fixed price, done in 72 hours, full refund if it doesn't save them 5+ hrs/month.
> Is that something [their company] could use? If not, do you know one owner who's drowning in admin? I'll send you $500 if they sign up.

## 2. Cold call (local business, 9am–5pm)

> **You:** Hi, is this the owner? My name's [Name], I'm local. Got 30 seconds? I'll be quick.
> **You:** I help [HVAC companies] stop losing jobs to slow follow-up. I noticed [a review mentioning no callback / no online booking]. Is following up on quotes something that slips through the cracks for you?
> *(If yes)* **You:** What's that cost you, roughly? How many quotes a week go unanswered?
> **You:** I set up an automation that texts and emails every quote within 5 minutes and follows up on day 2 and day 5, in the tools you already use. It's $2,500 fixed, live in 72 hours, and if it doesn't save you 5 hours a month I refund all of it. Want me to send you the link so I can start tonight?
> *(If hesitant)* **You:** Fair enough. Can I grab 15 minutes later today to show you exactly what it'd look like? [Cal.com link]

## 3. Voicemail (20 seconds)

> Hi [Name], it's [Name] at [number]. I help [industry] owners automate quote follow-ups so jobs stop slipping away. Fixed price, guaranteed. I'm taking 4 clients this week. Call or text me back at [number].

## 4. Cold email (publicly listed business address only, CASL-compliant)

**Subject:** [Company]'s quote follow-ups

> Hi [Name],
>
> I saw [specific detail]. Most [industry] shops lose 20–30% of quotes simply because no one follows up.
>
> I'll automate that for you in 72 hours (instant reply plus day-2 and day-5 follow-ups, in your current tools) for a fixed $2,500. If it doesn't save you 5+ hours a month, you get a full refund.
>
> Worth a 15-min call today? [Cal.com link]
>
> [Name] · [Phone] · [City]
> Reply "no thanks" and I won't contact you again.

## 5. Follow-up (same day, 5pm)

> Hi [Name], following up on this morning. I've got [N] of 4 spots left at this price, and I'm closing them tonight. Want one? Here's the link: [payment link]

## Objection handling

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "Too expensive" | "How many hours a week does this take now? At $X/hr that's $Y/month. This pays for itself in [N] weeks, and it's guaranteed." Or offer the $497 mini-offer. |
| "Send me info" | "Sure. What would you need to see to decide today? I'll send it now and call back in an hour." |
| "Need to think" | "Totally fair. What's the one thing you're unsure about?" |
| "Who are you?" | Be honest: "I'm new to doing this as a service. That's why there's a full refund guarantee and you can pay 60% now and 40% on delivery." |
