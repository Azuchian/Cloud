# 72-Hour Sprint Delivery Checklist

## Hour 0 (right after payment)
- [ ] Send a thank-you email and a 10-question intake form (Google Forms): tools they use, logins via their password manager share, FAQs, tone of voice
- [ ] Book a 30-min kickoff call for the next morning

## Day 1: Build
- [ ] Map the current workflow on one page: trigger → steps → outcome
- [ ] Build it in their stack using free tiers where you can: Zapier/Make free, n8n (self-hosted), Google Apps Script, their CRM's built-in automations
- [ ] Use Claude to draft message templates and replies in their tone
- [ ] Test with 5 real or dummy records

## Day 2: Launch
- [ ] Turn it on for live traffic
- [ ] Record a 5-min Loom walkthrough and write a 1-page "how to edit this" doc
- [ ] Measure the baseline: hours per week before vs. projected after

## Day 3: Hand-off
- [ ] Review call: show it working, collect the 40% balance if applicable
- [ ] Ask for a testimonial and 2 referrals ($500 referral bounty)
- [ ] Offer a $300/month maintenance plan (this is how a one-day $10K becomes recurring income)

## Common Sprint builds
1. **Quote follow-up:** form/email → CRM → instant SMS/email → day 2/5 reminders
2. **Invoice chasing:** overdue invoices in QuickBooks/Wave → polite reminder sequence
3. **Review requests:** job marked complete → review link by text after 2 hours
4. **Lead intake:** web form → qualify with AI → book on the calendar → notify the owner
5. **FAQ reply assistant:** shared inbox → AI-drafted replies for the owner to approve
